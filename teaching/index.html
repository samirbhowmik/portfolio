Teaching

Research-led courses and seminars, several developed in collaboration with biennials, museums, and partner institutions.

  • Ecological Thinking Seminar

    Academy of Fine Arts, University of the Arts Helsinki · Doctoral · 6 cr · 2021–present · Creator & lead instructor

  • Rare Earths: Field Studies in the Extractive North

    KUNO Network · MA · 5 cr · 2024–present · Co-creator

    A KUNO Network field-studies course examining mining landscapes and extractivism through artistic research.

    with NTNU Norway, Royal Institute of Art Stockholm

    Aalto University · 2018–2021 · Developed & taught

    A course on the environmental dimensions of media systems and infrastructures.

  • Archaeology of Media Infrastructures

    Aalto University · 2018–2021 · Developed & taught

  • MA & Doctoral Supervision

    University of the Arts Helsinki · 2021–present · Supervisor & advisor
