Service & Contributions

Editorial

  • Editorial Board Member Journal of Media Art, Study & Theory (MAST), SUNY · 2019–present

    Peer review (2–4 reviews annually) and proposals for special issues.

  • Environment, Data, Contamination Art Theoretical Writings #17 · 2023

    Co-edited with Jussi Parikka; connects environmental data, satellite sensing, and artistic practice with international contributors.

  • Unfolding Island Ecologies Art Theoretical Writings #23 · 2025

    Co-edited with Kati Kivinen and Blanca de la Torre, in collaboration with Helsinki Biennial 2025.

Research leadership

  • PI, Terra-Performing Research Group University of the Arts Helsinki · 2022–present

    Leading a team of a performance researcher and a data science researcher, producing peer-reviewed publications, conference presentations, and collaborative performances.

  • Facilitator, Ecological Thinking Research Group Academy of Fine Arts, Uniarts Helsinki · 2023–present

    Fostering interdisciplinary dialogue on extractivism and artistic practice across the Academy of Fine Arts.

International networks

  • Long-term collaboration with Prof. Jussi Parikka Aarhus University · 2017–present

    Joint publications (Leonardo, Acoustic Space Journal, edited volumes), artistic projects, and the Design & Aesthetics for Environmental Data research group.

  • Just-AI collaboration with Dr. Alison Powell London School of Economics

    Examined AI ethics through artistic practice.

  • KUNO Network teaching partnership NTNU Trondheim & Royal Institute of Art Stockholm

    Contributed to the Rare Earths field studies course with Prof. Nabil Ahmed.

Public engagement

  • Knowledge Exchange & Public Engagement

    Collaborations with Helsinki Biennial (2021, 2023, 2025), Venice Architecture Biennale (2021), IHME Helsinki (2021), and Museum of Impossible Forms (2024), engaging thousands of visitors and connecting artistic research with climate science.
