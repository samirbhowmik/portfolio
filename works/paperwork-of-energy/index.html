Paperwork of Energy (2021)

An installation tracing the bureaucratic and material substrates of energy systems, made with media theorist Jussi Parikka for the Venice Architecture Biennale.

installation · research · Venice Architecture Biennale · with Jussi Parikka

Paperwork of Energy excavates the documents, permits, and administrative residue through which energy infrastructures are governed and made invisible. The work sits at the seam between media archaeology and architecture: the paperwork is the infrastructure.
